Ancient Tree Quest

The team arrives at an ancient tree. Deep inside the trunk lies a termite tunnel, while woodpecker guards patrol above. Insect companions must handle the hidden passages inside, while bird companions keep the guards distracted outside. Only when all four key tasks succeed can the team safely enter the hollow tree.

Assign a suitable companion to perform the task.

Game Rules

Hover to view skill points.

Available companions are loaded from the previous program. Demo mode currently randomizes them.
  • Two bird tasks and two insect tasks.
  • At least one bird and one insect are required.
  • If only one same-type slot is filled, that companion checks both tasks.
  • If both same-type slots are filled, the other companion supports with half skill.
  • If another companion is assigned to the same type of slot, they can provide half of their skill as support.
Drag at least one bird and one insect into the matching task slots.
